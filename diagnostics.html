Pathology

Lab Tests

Blood and urine investigations to confirm a diagnosis, monitor an existing condition, or screen before a procedure.

Commonly includes
  • Complete blood count, sugar and thyroid profile
  • Hormonal panels for PCOS, fertility and menopause
  • Pregnancy-related and antenatal blood tests
  • Lipid profile and cardiac risk markers
  • Vitamin, iron and general health screening
At your doorstep

Home Collection

If travelling to the clinic is difficult, your sample can be collected at home and the reports shared with your doctor here.

Especially helpful for
  • Pregnancy, particularly the later months
  • Elderly patients and anyone with limited mobility
  • Recovery after surgery or a cardiac event
  • Routine follow-up tests between consultations

Imaging

Ultrasound

A painless scan that uses sound waves — no radiation involved — done on site so Dr. Roshana can review the images with you straight away.

Commonly used for
  • Pregnancy scans and monitoring your baby's growth
  • Pelvic scans for periods, pain and PCOS
  • Follicular study during fertility treatment
  • Abdominal ultrasound
Cardiac

ECG

A quick, painless recording of your heart's electrical activity. Small stickers are placed on the chest, arms and legs — it takes only a few minutes.

Usually advised for
  • Chest pain, breathlessness or palpitations
  • Checking heart rate and rhythm
  • Signs of strain on the heart muscle
  • Routine screening before surgery
Cardiac

ECHO

An ultrasound scan of the heart. It shows the chambers, valves and pumping function in motion — reviewed by Dr. Mukul, Consultant Cardiologist.

Helps assess
  • How well the heart is pumping
  • Valve narrowing or leakage
  • Breathlessness and swelling of the feet
  • Follow-up after a heart attack or heart failure
